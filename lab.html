Reading the spectrogram: time (years) runs left to right, frequency runs into depth, intensity is height. Each ridge is one partial of the sound. Straight, parallel ridges mean a harmonic, stable sound. Bent, irregular or blurred ridges show the effect of detune, inharmonicity and wavefolding. In the Bars view each bar is a year and each row, from back to front, is a frequency band (low to high). Bar height is the mean intensity of that band in that year, and the year playing is highlighted. Use the slider above to move through time.

The data and the strangeness derived from it

Top: submissions per year. Bottom: strangeness (0–1) computed with the current settings. Click a year to jump there.
